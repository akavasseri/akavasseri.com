About Me

Hi I’m Abhijna!! I’m currently a rising junior at Princeton University studying Electrical and Computer Engineering with a minor in Computer Science and a concentration in Computer Systems. Currently, I am working on hardware-software co-design projects as a hardware acceleration intern.

My interests are at the intersection of hardware and software. A few interests include: digital logic design, computer architecture, RTL design, and FPGAs. In my free time my projects lie more on the hardware consisting of analog audio, embedded systems, and FPGA game projects. My favorite passions of mine to mix are music and electrical engineering, which creates some of the coolest projects (you can see this in the projects tab!). Outside of my technical side I have many hobbies including: running, swimming, yoga, baking, reading Greek mythology, making ceremonial matcha, listening to EDM music, singing classical music and playing the piano.
